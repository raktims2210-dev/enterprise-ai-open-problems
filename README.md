# Enterprise AI: Open Problem Statements

This repository contains open problem statements derived from real enterprise-scale AI systems.
These are not implementation guides or solutions, but framing of challenges that remain
poorly understood at scale.

The intent is to enable academic discussion, student exploration, and practitioner dialogue
around long-lived, regulated, production-grade AI systems.

## Scope and Boundaries
- No proprietary code, data, or client references
- No claims of ownership over solutions
- Problems are framed abstractly and contextually

## Problem List
1. Why Enterprise AI systems fail after successful POCs
2. Cost explosion in large-scale LLM inference
3. Evaluation of AI systems under audit and regulatory constraints
4. Human-in-the-loop breakdowns at enterprise scale
5. Decision accountability in agentic AI systems
